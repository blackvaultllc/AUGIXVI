How to use it
  1. Tap Start Camera and allow camera access when the browser asks. The camera picture stays on screen the whole time — searching, locked, or lost.
  2. Hold the cube in front of the camera, one face pointing at the camera, about 1–2 feet away.
  3. Keep good light on the cube. When a face locks on, the model takes over the cube — the printed cube disappears and the model stands inside its space, in 3D. Turn the cube and it turns with it.
  4. Pick a model with the buttons, keys 1–4, or type what you want in the filter — try wolf, brain, skull, scanner. Built-in models are instant. Library models download once, only the one you pick (a few MB), then they are ready.
